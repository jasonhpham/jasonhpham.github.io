Japanese Salaryman (March 2026-)

Wishlist and Play the Demo!

Japanese Salaryman is an office-noir RPG set in 1990s Japan, where a debt-ridden security specialist balances everyday office life with corporate espionage. Players hack company systems, sell sensitive information, and navigate relationships and faction reputations to work their way out of debt.

As a gameplay programmer, I develop backend systems and authoring tools in Unity and C# for a modular relationship system. My work in runtime includes in-game relationship progression tracking, collectable tracking, and event-driven NPC spawn scheduling. For the backend, I extended database and editor tools so designers can configure character relationships (collectables, availability, art/scene assets).

As a game designer, I also contribute to feature refinement, working on gameplay balancing and side quests to support the game's progression and player experience.

BREAKER (Sept 2025-March 2026)

Play Here!

BREAKER is a speedrunning FPS made within Unreal Engine 5 where the only objectives are adrenaline and revenge. The intended experience is a fast-paced arena movement shooter with an emphasis on speedrunning.

As a combat gameplay programmer, I extended Unreal Engine 5's FPS template in C++ to turn designer prototypes and documents into playable weapon systems.

  • Weapon systems: Implemented hitscan and projectile firing, configurable recoil and bullet spread, ammo management, weapon inventory and switching, and respawning pickups.
  • Abilities and designer tools: Integrated weapon damage and discard-triggered "cashout" abilities with Unreal's Gameplay Ability System, exposing settings so designers could tune weapons and assign abilities directly in the editor.
  • UI implementation: Connected weapon and ammo state to HUD updates and used Adobe Photoshop to create UI assets from designer prototypes for implementation in-game.

The Murder of Master Roberts (March-June 2025)

The Murder of Master Roberts is a text-based murder mystery built in HTML and JavaScript using Twine where players take the role of a detective investigating the death of the eccentric billionaire Mr. Roberts. Players have the option to interrogate a cast of 7 different characters, gathering clues along the way and discerning red herrings to discover the true murderer.

As a gameplay programmer, I integrated the Google Sheets API in JavaScript to track player decisions, time spent, and paths taken across playthroughs. My team and I used this data to analyze player behavior in our game.

Grand Prix (October-December 2024)

Grand Prix is a bluffing and risk-taking card game for 3-6 players, with matches lasting 15-30 minutes. Inspired by the casual tension of Skull and Uno, players predict opponents' face-down cards in sequential 1v1 races, building their Prize Pool or backing down to cut their losses. Ties and special card abilities keep races unpredictable.

As a co-game designer, I contributed to the game's design and created all digital graphics, including the cards and cover art.

Landlord Simulator (March-June 2024)

Landlord Simulator is a management simulator, where players take on the role of well, a landlord. As a landlord, you have to balance factors such as tenant satisfaction, rent, upgrades, and the surprises life brings you (a pipe explosion). This game features tenant interactions, financial management, and a week-based progression system, along with randomly generated events to keep things engaging. With this game, our group sought out to make something as mundane as being a landlord, fun.

I contributed to game systems design and programming, implementing the game's systems in Unity.

Lost To Space (February-June 2024)

Lost to Space is a 2D survival sandbox, where you play as a marooned astronaut part of the Reconnaissance for Earth's Survival (R.E.S.) program. Players must navigate a procedurally generated, looping world, utilizing movement abilities, mining and placing blocks, managing inventory, engaging in combat, and facing enemies (big and small slimes!) to survive.

As a gameplay programmer, I implemented core mechanics including the movement system, enemy AI behavior, and combat framework.

Virtual Vendetta (February-March 2024)

Virtual Vendetta is a cyberpunk-inspired deck-building card battler, where your goal is to beat the champion of the metro. Standing in your way though, lays a path of enemies that you have to take down, each with their own deck designs and behaviors. As a player, you have a vast arsenal of cards that you can buy and customize your deck with, choosing either to duke it out on your own, or possibly set up deadly synergies with your partner. Virtual Vendetta supports local 2-player co-op, with the option for a single-player experience with an AI companion.

As a gameplay programmer, I coded all card interactions and types, AI, and status effects. I also contributed to the game's combat design.
